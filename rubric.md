# Call Grading Rubric (QA Lite, v1.0)

Each criterion is scored **0, 5, or 10**. Total per call: 0 to 50.
Calls may be in Spanish, English, or mixed. Language never affects scoring.

## C1. Greeting and Identification

- **10** : Agent greets, states company name AND their own name, and offers help, within the opening of the call.
- **5** : Greeting present but incomplete (missing own name or company name), or delivered late.
- **0** : No real greeting, or agent never identifies the company.

## C2. Needs Discovery

- **10** : Agent asks questions to understand the customer's situation before proposing anything (vehicle, coverage status, budget, what prompted the call), and confirms understanding.
- **5** : Some discovery, but jumps to a pitch or resolution with an incomplete picture.
- **0** : No discovery. Agent pitches or processes blindly.

## C3. Compliance and Disclosures

- **10** : Recording disclosure delivered near the start of the call ("this call is/may be recorded" or equivalent in either language), AND no prohibited statements anywhere (guaranteeing coverage outcomes, quoting a final binding price as certain before underwriting, giving legal advice).
- **5** : Disclosure present but late or mangled, and no prohibited statements.
- **0** : No recording disclosure, OR any prohibited statement, regardless of everything else.

## C4. Resolution and Next Steps

- **10** : The customer's reason for calling is resolved, or a concrete next step is set with a specific owner and timeframe ("I will call you tomorrow before noon with the quote").
- **5** : Partial resolution, or next step is vague ("we'll be in touch").
- **0** : Call ends with no resolution and no next step, or the promised action contradicts what was discussed.

## C5. Professionalism and Call Control

- **10** : Courteous throughout, lets the customer finish, keeps the call on track, handles frustration without escalating it.
- **5** : Generally professional with lapses (talking over the customer, losing the thread, excessive dead air or filler).
- **0** : Rude, dismissive, argumentative, or completely loses control of the call.

## Notes for graders

- Score what is in the transcript. Do not infer off-transcript behavior.
- Each criterion is independent. A call can score 0 on C3 and 10 on everything else.
- When a call is too short for a criterion to apply meaningfully, grade what the agent did with the opportunity they had.
